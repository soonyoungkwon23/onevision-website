Common questions

If your question isn't here, leave it in the contact form.

Will you write my assignment for me?

No. We don't write assignments, essays or theses, and we don't take exams. We give feedback on work you wrote and help you through the parts where you're stuck.

When can I have sessions?

Weekday evenings and weekends in Korea time, and weekday evenings in U.S. Pacific time. We'll find exact slots on the call based on coach availability.

Which subjects do you cover?

Mostly undergraduate and master's courses in business, accounting, statistics, nursing, psychology, education, computing and English composition. If yours isn't listed, we'll check whether we have a coach for it.

How does payment work?

You pay before each session. A 10-session pack can be used over three months, and unused sessions are refunded. Tutoring starts at $50 a session.

What happens on the free call?

In 15 minutes we hear about your school or program, the credits you have left and your most urgent deadline. Within two days you get a plan and an estimated cost, and then you decide whether to go ahead.

Is outside editing allowed at my school?

Most schools allow tutoring and editing feedback. Rules can differ by course, so we read the assignment instructions with you, and if it's unclear we'll suggest asking your instructor first.

Do you help with Korea's Credit Bank System?

Yes. If you're finishing a bachelor's through the Credit Bank or the self-study degree exams, we map out the credits you need, the yearly limits and the degree application windows. You enrol with the institution yourself, and we help you compare options.

Do I need to live in the U.S.?

No. Every session is online, so you can join from Korea, the U.S. or anywhere else.

What if my coach isn't a good fit?

If the first session isn't right, you don't pay for it and we match you with someone else. You can switch any time after that too.

Is what I share kept private?

Your writing, transcripts and anything you tell us are seen only by your coach. We don't share them with your school or anyone else.
