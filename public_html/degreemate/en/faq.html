Common questions

If your question isn't here, send it on KakaoTalk or through the contact form.

Will you write my assignment for me?

No. We don't write assignments, essays or theses, and we don't take exams. We give feedback on work you wrote and help you through the parts where you're stuck.

Can I use AI tools?

Rules on AI tools differ by school and by course, so we check yours before giving any guidance. We never produce writing for you with AI. How to check your rules

I'm worried about plagiarism.

We practice citation and paraphrasing together, using writing you drafted, and check where sources are needed. We don't teach ways around plagiarism checkers.

Is outside editing allowed at my school?

Most schools allow tutoring and editing feedback. Rules can differ by course, so we read the assignment instructions with you, and if it's unclear we'll suggest asking your instructor first.

How does payment work?

You pay before each session. A 10-session pack can be used over three months, and unused sessions are refunded. Tutoring starts at $50 a session. Refund policy

What happens on the free call?

In 15 minutes we hear about your school or program and your most urgent assignment or deadline. Within two days you get a plan and an estimated cost, and then you decide whether to go ahead.

Which subjects do you cover?

Mostly undergraduate and master's courses in business, accounting, statistics, nursing, psychology, education, computing and English composition. If yours isn't listed, we'll check whether we have a tutor for it.

When can I have sessions?

Every session is online, so you can join from Korea, the U.S. or anywhere else. Slots run on weekday evenings and weekends in Korea time and weekday evenings in U.S. Pacific time; we set exact times on the call.

What if my tutor isn't a good fit?

If the first session isn't right, you don't pay for it and we match you with someone else. You can switch any time after that too.

Is what I share kept private?

Your writing, transcripts and anything you tell us are seen only by your tutor. We don't share them with your school or anyone else.
