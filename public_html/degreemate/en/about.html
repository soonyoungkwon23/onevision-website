Who we are and how we work

DegreeMate is a new study-support brand from OneVision Consulting, which advises Korean families on U.S. study and immigration. We help people who work and study at the same time finish their degree.

How we work

  • Coaches either studied the subject they teach or work in the field.
  • Sessions run in English or Korean, whichever you prefer.
  • Only your coach sees the writing and transcripts you send, and we don't use them for anything else.
  • Session notes are kept so you can look back at them any time.

Coaches

Coach profiles go up when the full site launches. If you book a call now, we'll send you the background of the coach who fits your subject first.

Academic integrity

Most schools allow outside tutoring and editing feedback, but the limits vary by school and by course. If an assignment or your school's policy restricts outside help, we follow it. When it's unclear, we'll ask you to check with your instructor first.

We won't do the following even if asked, and repeated requests end the service.

What we do

  • Explain concepts and work through problems with you
  • Give feedback on writing you drafted yourself
  • Break a thesis into steps and check progress weekly
  • Sort out transfer credit and compare schools
  • Practice presentations, office-hour talks and emails in English

What we don't do

  • Write assignments, essays or theses for you
  • Take exams or quizzes in your place
  • Log in and attend online classes for you
  • Show ways around plagiarism checks
  • Anything else your school's rules forbid
