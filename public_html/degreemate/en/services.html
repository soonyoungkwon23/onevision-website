Essay and assignment feedback

Essays, reports, lab reports, discussion posts

Format
Written feedback
Turnaround
5 days, or 48 hours
Price
from $45 / 1,000 words

You send a draft you wrote. We comment on structure, argument, citations and English, paragraph by paragraph, with examples of how to revise. We also practice citation and paraphrasing with you. You do the revising.

What you get

  • Comments on the structure and argument of the whole draft
  • Paragraph comments and revision examples in tracked changes
  • A citation check (APA, MLA and others) and paraphrasing practice
  • An optional 15-minute call to go over the feedback

Good fit if

You know what you want to say but it won't come out on the page, or you're unsure of your English.

Format
4 sessions a month
Term
Monthly
Price
from $320 / month

We break the work into small steps, from choosing a topic and designing the study to structure feedback, and check progress with you every week. You do the writing; we keep the schedule and direction on track.

What you get

  • A step-by-step schedule worked back from your submission date
  • Feedback on your topic and study design
  • One 50-minute check-in a week and feedback on each chapter
  • Help preparing for meetings with your advisor

Good fit if

You have a topic but haven't moved in months, or you're writing a thesis around a full-time job.
