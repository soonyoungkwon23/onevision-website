Service details

How it works

  1. Free 15-minute call

    Tell us where you are, what's due and what you're aiming for. Phone, video or KakaoTalk, whichever is easiest.

  2. Get your plan

    Within two days you get one page with the credits left, a timeline, the help you need and the expected cost.

  3. Meet your coach

    We match you with someone who knows the subject and fits your hours. If the first session isn't a fit, you don't pay for it.

  4. Weekly check-ins

    Each session ends with your next steps written down, and we adjust the plan at the end of every term.

What we do and what we don't

Your school decides how much outside help is allowed, and we stay inside those rules.

What we do

  • Explain concepts and work through problems with you
  • Give feedback on writing you drafted yourself
  • Break a thesis into steps and check progress weekly
  • Sort out transfer credit and compare schools
  • Practice presentations, office-hour talks and emails in English

What we don't do

  • Write assignments, essays or theses for you
  • Take exams or quizzes in your place
  • Log in and attend online classes for you
  • Show ways around plagiarism checks
  • Anything else your school's rules forbid

Common questions

Will you write my assignment for me?

No. We don't write assignments, essays or theses, and we don't take exams. We give feedback on work you wrote and help you through the parts where you're stuck.

When can I have sessions?

Weekday evenings and weekends in Korea time, and weekday evenings in U.S. Pacific time. We'll find exact slots on the call based on coach availability.

Which subjects do you cover?

Mostly undergraduate and master's courses in business, accounting, statistics, nursing, psychology, education, computing and English composition. If yours isn't listed, we'll check whether we have a coach for it.

How does payment work?

You pay before each session. A 10-session pack can be used over three months, and unused sessions are refunded. Tutoring starts at $50 a session.
